Fire Resistant Cable Sealing Compound

A Class certified, fire-resistant, air tight & water tight cable transit duct/hole sealing compound for achieving fire proof, gas & water tight cable transits in decks & bulkheads as to type A60 SOLAS requirements. The sealing compound is suitable for all types of cables used in shipbuilding

The sealing system effectively prevents the spread of fire, smoke, gas, and water through cable transits, thereby enhancing the overall safety of the vessel and protecting both crew and equipment. It is suitable for use with all categories and sizes of electrical and communication cables commonly used in shipbuilding and offshore constructions.
