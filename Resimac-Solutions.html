RESIMAC Solutions

Resimac engineering repair materials and coatings are used globally by some of the largest companies worldwide. Our products are proven to repair, upgrade and protect key process equipment and site infrastructure, ensuring reduced downtime for key assets. From pumps and valves to storage tanks to large diameter transmission lines our products can resist harsh industrial chemicals and varied operating conditions, even at elevated temperatures. Our products have been designed to be applied using standard application techniques, such as airless spray rigs and are compatible with international QC standards to ensure a seamless chemical resistant finish.

To help us in recommending the most suitable product, kindly do get in touch with us by email or phone with the application details.
