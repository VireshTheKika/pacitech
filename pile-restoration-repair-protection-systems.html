Services

Pile Restoration, Repair and Protection Systems

Pacitech Chemical Engg Pvt Ltd. engineering repair materials and coatings are used globally by some of the largest chemical companies worldwide. Our products are proven to repair, upgrade and protect key process equipment and site infrastructure, ensuring reduced downtime for key assets.

From pumps and valves to storage tanks to large diameter transmission lines our products can resist harsh industrial chemicals such as 98% sulphuric acid, even at elevated temperatures. Our products have been designed to be appplied using standard application techniques, such as airless spray rigs and are compatible with international QC standards to ensure a seamless chemical resistant finish.
